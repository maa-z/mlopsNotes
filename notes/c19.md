
No loss function.

# 28. Interview Questions

Q1. What is artifact-driven inference?

Answer:

An inference system that reconstructs behavior entirely from saved artifacts rather than hardcoded values.

---

Q2. Why load config.json?

Answer:

To recreate architecture and inference settings.

---

Q3. Why create model before loading weights?

Answer:

Weights need a matching architecture to be loaded into.

---

Q4. Why add a batch dimension?

Answer:

Models expect input shape:

```python
[B,C,H,W]
```

---

Q5. Why load class_mapping.json?

Answer:

To convert class indices into human-readable labels.

---

Q6. Why use Softmax during inference?

Answer:

To convert logits into probabilities.

---

Q7. Why is artifact-driven inference superior?

Answer:

It is scalable, reusable, configurable, and production-ready.
