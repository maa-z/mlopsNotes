# 27. Interview Questions

Q1. What is a model artifact?

Answer:

Any asset required to use, reproduce, or deploy a model.

---

Q2. Why is a .pth file alone insufficient?

Answer:

It contains weights but lacks configuration, mappings, and metadata.

---

Q3. Why save class_mapping.json?

Answer:

To convert model output indices into human-readable labels.

---

Q4. Why save config.json?

Answer:

To recreate model architecture and inference settings.

---

Q5. Why save metadata.json?

Answer:

For tracking, auditing, debugging, and governance.

---

Q6. What is artifact-driven inference?

Answer:

Inference configured entirely from saved artifacts rather than hardcoded values.

---

Q7. Why version artifacts?

Answer:

To support reproducibility and rollback.
