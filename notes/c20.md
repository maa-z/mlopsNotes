# 26. Interview Questions

Q1. What is MLOps?

Answer:

The discipline of managing the lifecycle of machine learning systems from development to deployment and monitoring.

---

Q2. Why are artifacts important?

Answer:

They package all information required for reproducible inference and deployment.

---

Q3. Why store models in S3?

Answer:

Durability, versioning, sharing, and deployment readiness.

---

Q4. Why separate training and inference?

Answer:

They have different goals, workflows, and requirements.

---

Q5. What is the biggest difference between a notebook project and a production system?

Answer:

Production systems prioritize reproducibility, deployment, scalability, monitoring, and reliability.

---

Q6. Why is validation critical?

Answer:

It measures generalization on unseen data.

---

Q7. What is artifact-driven inference?

Answer:

Reconstructing a deployable model from saved artifacts rather than hardcoded values.
