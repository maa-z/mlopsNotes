
```python

 'layer1.bias': ...,

 'layer2.weight': ...,
```

```text

Biases

Trainable Parameters
```

---

Does NOT contain:

```text

Images

Training Loop
```

---

Only model state.

---

# 26. Interview Questions

Q1. What is state_dict()?

Answer:

A dictionary containing model parameters and buffers.

---

Q2. What does torch.save() do?

Answer:

Serializes and saves a PyTorch object to disk.

---

Q3. What is stored in best_model.pth?

Answer:

In our case, the model's state_dict (weights and related tensors).

---

Q4. Does state_dict contain architecture?

Answer:

No.

Only parameters and buffers.

---

Q5. Why create architecture before loading?

Answer:

The saved parameters need a matching model structure to be loaded into.

---

Q6. Why is state_dict preferred over saving the whole model?

Answer:

More portable, reliable, and less dependent on code structure.

---

Q7. Why move artifacts to S3?

Answer:

Durable storage, versioning, sharing, and deployment readiness.
